# Frozen splits for retraining

Generated by `python -m training.make_splits` (seed 42). One row per image in `manifest.csv`.

Columns:
- `path`: relative to the project root.
- `split`: `train`, `val` or `test`.
- `class_key`: the training class (subject + ear side when known). Empty for test rows.
- UBEAR only: `session`, `pose` and `sequence` (one video).

**Test subjects are disjoint from training subjects in every dataset.** Never train on `split == test`.

Notes:
- **UERC / NITJ:** the official splits are kept. Their `val` shares subjects with `train` (a closed-set check). Their `test` subjects are held out.
- **UBEAR:** 30% of subjects train, stratified by gender and session-2 capture. 6 of those subjects form a subject-disjoint `val`. The other 70% are `test`. Training and validation keep at most 5 evenly spaced frames per video; test keeps every frame.
- **AWE:** only the 11 subjects in UERC's test split. The other 89 AWE subjects are UERC training data.
- **ITW:** test only (no training part is available).
- **Suggested batch mix for the joint fine-tune:** about 60% UERC, 20% NITJ, 20% UBEAR by sampling share (raw counts would leave UBEAR under 1%).
